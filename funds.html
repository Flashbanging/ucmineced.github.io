UC Mineced is a community server for UC Merced students. Nobody here is trying to make a profit.

Our promise

All money received from rank purchases is put right back into UC Mineced. Staff are not paid from the store. If there is ever more money than the server needs, it goes toward better performance, new features, and community events.

What it pays for

  • Server hosting: the machine that keeps the world online and running smoothly.
  • Backups: so a crash or a griefer never costs you your build.
  • Plugins and tools: the software that powers homes, auctions, warps and claims.
  • Domain and website: the address you type to join, and this store.
  • Events and prizes: build contests and giveaways for the community.

Why ranks are cheap

We're students too. Prices are set so anyone who wants to help can, and one purchase lasts for good. Guardian is completely free with /freerank, so you never need to pay to enjoy the server. Ranks add convenience, not power over other players.

Questions

Ask staff in the server Discord or in game. We're happy to explain anything about how funds are used.
